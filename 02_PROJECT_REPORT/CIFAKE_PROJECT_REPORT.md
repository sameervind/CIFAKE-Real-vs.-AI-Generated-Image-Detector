## 1. Abstract
The rapid maturation of text-to-image latent diffusion models (e.g., Stable Diffusion, Midjourney, DALL-E) has undermined trust in digital imagery. Automated, mathematically defensible media authentication systems are critically required. This project presents **CIFAKE**, an industrial-grade, CPU-optimized computer vision platform designed to classify genuine photographic imagery versus synthetic AI-generated media. 

Following an exhaustive forensic audit of legacy transfer-learning baselines—which suffered from destructive spatial upsampling, massive parameter bloat, uncalibrated heuristic overrides, and out-of-distribution (OOD) conflation—we engineer a native $32 \times 32$ deep convolutional neural network (`CIFAR_CNN`). Operating with only **305,953 trainable parameters** (a 79-fold reduction over ResNet-50), the system achieves an independently verified **94.92% accuracy**, **0.9492 macro F1-score**, and **0.9903 ROC-AUC** across the official 20,000-image CIFAKE test partition. Running at **1.58 ms per image CPU latency** (a 26.6-fold speedup), the neural architecture is integrated with an Out-of-Distribution Manifold Arbiter and supplementary 2D Discrete Fourier Transform (2D FFT) frequency diagnostics. The platform completely eliminates false-positive AI classifications on verified authentic human portraits while maintaining 100% detection on modern synthetic regression specimens.

## 2. Introduction
Digital image authenticity has become a central challenge in modern computer vision and cybersecurity. Historically, synthetic imagery generated by Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) exhibited telltale structural artifacts such as facial asymmetry, blurred boundary contours, and warping. However, modern denoising diffusion probabilistic models (DDPMs) operate via iterative latent denoising, producing photorealistic textures, coherent global lighting, and fine detail that routinely deceive human observers.

The CIFAKE benchmark dataset was established to evaluate binary authenticity detection, containing 60,000 real CIFAR-10 photographs and 60,000 synthetic images generated using Stable Diffusion v1.4 across ten matched semantic categories. Despite its utility, applying off-the-shelf deep neural networks to CIFAKE frequently introduces subtle engineering defects:
1. Spatial resolution mismatch between small benchmark images ($32 \times 32$) and large ImageNet backbones ($224 \times 224$).
2. Training/inference preprocessing divergences.
3. Uncalibrated heuristic overrides in production decision engines.
4. Epistemic failure when handling real-world out-of-distribution imagery (such as high-resolution portraits).

This report documents the end-to-end diagnosis, architectural redesign, empirical validation, and full-stack software delivery of the stabilized CIFAKE detection platform.

## 4. Existing System & Critical Failure Audit
An exhaustive source-code and notebook audit of legacy CIFAKE implementations uncovered critical architectural and statistical failures:

1. **Receptive Field Mismatch & Destructive Upsampling:**
   Legacy pipelines upscaled native $32 \times 32$ images to $224 \times 224$ via bilinear interpolation to satisfy standard ResNet-50 input dimensions. This caused a $49\times$ spatial memory inflation, introduced severe interpolation blur, and attenuated microscopic high-frequency diffusion artifacts. Furthermore, ResNet-50's aggressive stem ($7 \times 7$ conv with stride 2 followed by $3 \times 3$ max pool with stride 2) reduced $32 \times 32$ images to $8 \times 8$ before Stage 1, obliterating 75% of spatial resolution in the first two operations.
2. **Untrained Heuristic Decision Overrides:**
   The legacy forensic engine calculated synthetic probabilities using handcrafted combinations of 2D FFT spectral peaks, off-axis power, and Laplacian kurtosis. An arbitrary override `spectral_peak > 6.5` independently forced an AI verdict, causing sharp authentic photographs to be classified as AI with 98% false confidence.
3. **Out-of-Distribution (OOD) Conflation:**
   High-resolution natural images naturally deviated from the low-resolution CIFAR-10 manifold. The legacy OOD detector converted high OOD distance scores directly into synthetic probabilities, penalizing genuine photographs as synthetic.
4. **Data Leakage in Experimental Notebooks:**
   The original exploration notebook executed data augmentation across the entire 120,000-image dataset *before* performing train/test splits, contaminating evaluation partitions.
5. **Model Identity & Confidence Fabrication:**
   The codebase claimed to execute a 24-million parameter ResNet-50 while actually loading a 305k-parameter native CNN. Furthermore, dashboard cards displayed hardcoded 98.0% confidence and static metrics rather than live model predictions.

## 5. Proposed System Overview
To resolve these failures, we engineered a unified, multi-tiered forensic detection architecture:

1. **Native Spatial Receptive Field (`CIFAR_CNN`):**
   A dedicated 6-layer convolutional architecture designed specifically for $32 \times 32$ input geometry. Uses $3 \times 3$ filters with stride 1, Batch Normalization, progressive Dropout (0.2 to 0.4), and Global Average Pooling into a 128-dimensional dense manifold embedding.
2. **Unified Model-Internal Preprocessing:**
   The normalization operation (`layers.Rescaling(1.0 / 255.0)`) is internalized directly into the Keras model graph. Both training and inference pipelines accept raw $[0, 255]$ uint8 arrays, guaranteeing zero preprocessing divergence.
3. **Decoupled OOD Manifold Gating:**
   The Out-of-Distribution engine monitors spatial scale and feature distance relative to the training manifold. OOD telemetry acts as an epistemic uncertainty metric that discounts decision confidence without flipping class labels.
4. **Supplementary Optical Diagnostics:**
   2D Discrete Fourier Transform (2D FFT) radial power fall-off, sensor PRNU noise variance, and Laplacian kurtosis are stripped of decision authority and retained strictly as explanatory telemetry for forensic analysts.
5. **Production Software Engineering:**
   FastAPI REST backend with OpenAPI documentation, a Lamborghini-inspired Streamlit dashboard with dynamic Aceternity Bento Grid components, and a comprehensive Pytest test suite with 100% pass rate.

## 7. Dataset Description & Preprocessing Pipeline

### 7.1 Dataset Composition
The CIFAKE benchmark dataset consists of 120,000 color images:
- **Class 0 (AI / Synthetic):** 60,000 images generated via Stable Diffusion v1.4.
- **Class 1 (Real Photography):** 60,000 authentic photographs from CIFAR-10.
- **Spatial Resolution:** $32 \times 32$ pixels, 3 color channels (RGB).
- **Semantic Classes (10 balanced categories):** Airplane, Automobile, Bird, Cat, Deer, Dog, Frog, Horse, Ship, Truck.

### 7.2 Strict Split Partitioning
- **Training Set:** 100,000 images (50,000 Real, 50,000 AI).
- **Test Set:** 20,000 images (10,000 Real, 10,000 AI) — strictly preserved, unaugmented, and held out for final verification.
- **Training Validation Split:** 80,000 train / 20,000 validation during model fitting.

### 7.3 Anti-Leakage Preprocessing
- **Internalized Rescaling:** Pixel values in $[0, 255]$ are divided by 255.0 inside the first Keras layer.
- **Physics-Preserving Augmentation:** Training augmentation is restricted to random horizontal reflection ($p=0.5$) and 4-pixel reflection padding with random cropping. Vertical flipping is strictly prohibited to preserve natural gravitational lighting.

## 9. Methodology
1. **Feature Extraction:** Spatial convolutions capture localized multi-scale pixel dependencies.
2. **Statistical Pooling:** Global Average Pooling reduces spatial dimensions $(4 \times 4 \times 128)$ to a single 128-dimensional embedding vector, enforcing translational invariance.
3. **Regularization:** Progressive dropout ($0.2 \to 0.3 \to 0.4$) and L2 weight decay ($10^{-4}$) prevent co-adaptation of weights on low-resolution textures.
4. **Calibrated Decision Arbitration:** The decision engine fuses the raw deep network output with OOD manifold distance:
   $$\text{Confidence} = \begin{cases} P(\text{REAL}) & \text{if } P(\text{REAL}) \ge 0.50 \\ 1 - P(\text{REAL}) & \text{if } P(\text{REAL}) < 0.50 \end{cases}$$
   When domain shift is detected ($\text{OOD} > 0.50$), confidence is discounted by a factor proportional to domain divergence, preventing overconfident assertions on unseen domains.

## 14. Limitations & Real-World Generalization Boundaries
1. **Benchmark Resolution Discrepancy:** The CIFAKE benchmark is trained at $32 \times 32$. While effective on downsampled images, ultra-high-resolution images (4K) contain macro-textures that differ from low-resolution training data.
2. **Recompression Artifacts:** Severe JPEG compression (quality factor $< 50$) attenuates high-frequency spectral fingerprints, reducing the signal-to-noise ratio of frequency-domain diagnostics.
3. **Novel Generative Architectures:** New models incorporating flow-matching or autoregressive transformers (e.g., FLUX, Midjourney v6) produce novel noise residuals requiring periodic model retraining.
